THE APP (gn-math portable fork)
  Single-file Wisp-proxy web desktop. Opens remote sites through Wisp servers:
    wss://arctic.lat/_q/          wss://wisp.mercurywork.shop/wisp/
    wss://arctic.lat/wisp/        wss://wisp.mercurywork.shop/
    wss://api.ixl.college/ws      wss://api.sngdu.com/ws
    wss://anura.pro/
  Engine: libcurl.js (LGPL) + Chrome-fingerprint transport, 15 accent themes,
  light/dark mode, clock, localStorage + IndexedDB session save, history/back/reload.
